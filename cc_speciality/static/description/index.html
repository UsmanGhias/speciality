Structured Skill & Qualification Directory

When dealing with hundreds of contractors, suppliers, consultants, or professional partners, maintaining a standard categorization of who does what is critical. Generic tags often get duplicated or misspelled.

The cc_speciality module provides an independent, clean registry of verified business specialities with unique alphanumeric codes, drag-and-drop sequence ordering, descriptions, and translation support. Contacts can be assigned multiple specialities, enabling quick searching and filtering across partner directories.

Core Capabilities

Centralized Speciality Catalog

Maintain a controlled registry of specialities with unique technical codes, sequence priority, and full descriptive documentation.

Partner Expertise Tagging

Assign specialities directly onto customer, vendor, or contractor records using interactive tags with full link navigation.

Search & Filter by Speciality

Filter contacts by specific business qualifications or domain skills directly in the standard partner directory search view.

Safe Archiving & Translations

Archive obsolete qualifications without breaking historical data or foreign key associations. Fully translatable for multi-lingual deployments.
